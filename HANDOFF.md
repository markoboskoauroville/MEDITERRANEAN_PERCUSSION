# HANDOFF — MEDITERRANEAN_PERCUSSION

**State: complete and published.** Third in the Mantra field-book series.

## Decisions worth not re-litigating

1. **Zohar Fresco has his own chapter.** The original plan named only Velez,
   Redmond and Afterman. Fresco is Afterman's teacher and the person through
   whom modern lapstyle was systematised — omitting him would have broken the
   lineage. Chapter four says openly that he was missing from the first plan.
2. **Chapter five declares the conflict of interest in its first sentence**
   and links everything to yshaiafterman.com. This is deliberate. A chapter
   about your own teacher that hides the relationship is worth nothing; one
   that declares it and cites sources is worth a great deal more, to the
   reader and to the teacher.
3. **Redmond's thesis is presented, then the objection is presented, then it
   is left open.** The iconography is solid; the prehistoric-matriarchy
   framework is contested. Do not resolve this in either direction.
4. **The djembe chapter compares rather than surveys.** Solo-and-ornament
   versus ensemble-and-occasion. It is not an attempt at Malinke coverage.
5. **Three captions were rewritten to match what the model painted**, rather
   than repainting to match the captions. Plate 11 was repainted because it
   duplicated plate 09.
